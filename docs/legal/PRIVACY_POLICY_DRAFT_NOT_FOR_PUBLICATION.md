## 2. Information we may process

Depending on the features you use and the final verified implementation, SAMURA may process:

- Account information such as name, email address, profile image and account status.
- Authentication and security data such as session identifiers, session expiry, IP address, browser/user-agent information and password-reset verification data.
- Prompts, conversation history, messages, uploaded files, documents and AI-generated outputs that you choose to submit or create.
- Connected-service information, including GitHub account identifiers, granted permissions and tokens required to provide an integration.
- Service usage metadata such as model/provider identifiers, token counts, request status and estimated usage costs.
- Technical logs, error reports and abuse-prevention records.
- Communications you send to support or through service email workflows.
- Cookie, device or analytics information if such technologies are enabled in the deployed service.

The final policy must list only categories actually collected and describe any additional categories accurately.

## 3. Why we process information

Subject to confirmation of the actual implementation and applicable law, purposes may include:

- Creating and securing accounts and sessions.
- Providing chat, document and AI functionality requested by users.
- Connecting to third-party services when users authorise an integration.
- Maintaining service reliability, preventing abuse and investigating security incidents.
- Responding to support requests and delivering necessary service communications.
- Measuring service usage, troubleshooting failures and managing service costs.
- Meeting legal obligations and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims where applicable.

The final policy must state the applicable lawful basis for each purpose under the laws that apply. Consent must not be described as the basis for processing unless valid consent is actually obtained and can be withdrawn where required.

## 4. AI processing and external providers

The exact providers, data fields, regions, retention rules, subprocessors and training settings must be confirmed from the production configuration and current contracts before publication.

Do not assume that a provider does not retain prompts or use them for training merely because an API is used. Do not promise zero retention, no training, exclusive processing or a specific geographic location without evidence.

The final version should link to or identify the relevant provider disclosures where appropriate and explain which user inputs may be transmitted.
